FAQs

Questions school teams often ask.

Which platforms can you manage?

Our social media service can cover Instagram, Facebook and Google Business Profile, based on the agreed scope.

Can you shoot professional videos at our school?

Yes. We can discuss an on-campus shoot for school introductions, classroom experiences, activities, events and short-form video content.

Do you help with admission enquiries?

We can plan admission-focused content and paid campaigns designed to encourage relevant parents to enquire. Results depend on the offer, location, budget and follow-up process.

Can you build a website or school ERP?

Yes. We can discuss school websites, enquiry forms, ERP workflows and parent communication app requirements.

How do we get a proposal?

Send us your institute name, location and the services you need. We’ll use that information to start a conversation about your goals and next steps.
